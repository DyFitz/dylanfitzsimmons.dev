What happens to your message

It's saved on this site and emailed to me, and only used to reply to you. Nothing is added to a mailing list or passed on to anyone else.

To stop spam, a scrambled form of your IP address is kept with it. That can't be turned back into the address, and it's only used to limit how many messages can be sent in an hour.

Messages are deleted after 12 months. To have yours deleted sooner, reply to my email and say so.
